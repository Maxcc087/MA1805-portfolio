This emoji I create is call, "After Rage Bait". I got the idea of creating this emoji from the reaction of both me and my friends rage baiting each other and famous Youtuber IshowSpeed's reaction after hearing one kid saying "Speed, I need this, my mama kinda homeless". Basically, this emoji is representing yourself trying your best to not laugh but you can't hold it. I think it is a very good emoji to use after making someone in a group chat rage and you close your eyes, trying your best to hold your laugh.
